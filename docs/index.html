Listening to a proton tunnel

A proton in a model double well, watched and heard. Hydrogen, deuterium, and tritium share one potential. The exact clock is a matrix exponential. Moth's engines measure that clock, blur a picture of the crossing, colour the state, and give the rhythm a room.
